Sprites are generated from part rigs in tools/art/horde-gen.mjs and landed into playground.html as text rows; the four skins are the same rows under a different palette, with the violet eyes turning ember. The grounds are the game's own baked chunks. Mob speeds, the spitter's range and the hop timing match the game; there is no damage here, only the crowd.
